Background

Built on the ground, refined at the top firms.

Before real estate, Rob spent more than a decade in construction and renovation, working as a project manager responsible for schedules, specifications, budgets and quality control, and on the tools himself on residential and commercial projects. He also worked in client service and finance at CIBC. That hands-on background still shapes how he evaluates a building: he looks past the brochure to clear height, shipping, power, structure and the true cost of making a space work.

Rob entered commercial real estate in 2014 at Binswanger Hectare, then advanced through Cushman & Wakefield and Colliers International, where he spent five years as a Senior Sales Representative on an industrial team marketing and leasing freestanding buildings in Mississauga and across the GTA. In December 2022 he joined Lennard Commercial Realty, Brokerage, where he is now Vice President.
