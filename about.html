About RGJ Commercial

Robert Johnson

GTA Industrial Sale & Leasing Specialist · Vice President, Sales Representative, Lennard Commercial Realty, Brokerage

Since 2014, Rob has helped business owners, tenants, landlords and investors buy, sell and lease industrial buildings across the Greater Toronto Area, completing more than 200 transactions totalling over 2 million square feet and more than $1 billion in value. RGJ Commercial is his advisory practice: one experienced advisor, personally involved from the first conversation to the closing, backed by Lennard's research and reach.
