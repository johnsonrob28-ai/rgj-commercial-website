Common questions

What does a buyer representative do?

A buyer representative works for the purchaser: defining the requirement, finding on- and off-market options, analyzing pricing, negotiating the agreement of purchase and sale and coordinating due diligence through to closing.

How is a buyer representative paid?

In many industrial sales the buyer's brokerage is paid from the commission offered by the seller, but this varies by transaction. Terms are confirmed in a written buyer representation agreement before work begins.

How long does it take to buy an industrial building?

Searching can take weeks to several months depending on supply for your size and area. Once an offer is accepted, conditional periods for financing, inspections and environmental review commonly run 30 to 90 days before closing.

Can you find buildings that are not for sale?

Often, yes. Many industrial buildings trade privately. When a requirement calls for it, RGJ Commercial approaches owners of suitable buildings directly.

Should I buy or lease?

It depends on how long you plan to stay, your capital and financing, and how specialized your space needs are. A side-by-side cost comparison over your expected holding period is the best place to start.
