Industrial property search questions

What information should I provide for an industrial property search?

Start with your target cities, size range, sale or lease preference and occupancy date. Add clear height, truck-level and drive-in doors, power, parking and any yard or outdoor storage needs.

How should I compare industrial lease costs?

Compare net rent together with additional rent, utilities, fit-out, moving costs and any incentives. Confirm which costs are estimates and which obligations are assigned to the tenant.
