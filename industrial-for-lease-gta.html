Before You Tour

What to check in a GTA industrial building.

Clear height & shipping

Usable clear height, truck-level and drive-in doors, dock levellers, trailer parking and truck court depth determine how efficiently you can move goods.

Power & building systems

Amperage and voltage, sprinkler type, lighting, heating and any make-up air for production. Upgrades can be expensive and slow, so confirm early.

Zoning & total cost

Confirm your use is permitted and whether outdoor storage is allowed. Then compare total occupancy cost, not just net rent: TMI, utilities, fit-out and moving.

GTA industrial leasing questions

What is the average industrial lease rate in the GTA?

Colliers reported an average net asking rent of $16.04 per square foot per year for the Toronto industrial market in Q3 2026. Net asking rent excludes additional rent (TMI), and actual rents vary widely by city, building age, clear height and unit size, so compare specific buildings rather than the market average.

What is TMI on an industrial lease?

TMI stands for taxes, maintenance and insurance. It is the additional rent a tenant pays on top of net rent in most GTA industrial leases. Ask for the current-year TMI estimate in writing and what it includes, such as property management fees or roof and structural reserves.

How far ahead should I start looking for industrial space?

For most requirements, start 12 to 18 months before you need to occupy. Larger or highly specialized requirements, and businesses that need to negotiate a renewal against relocation options, should start earlier.

Should I renew my lease or relocate?

Compare the full cost of each option, including rent, TMI, moving, fit-out, downtime and incentives, against how well the building supports your operation. Testing the market before renewal discussions usually improves the terms on either path.

Does it cost a tenant anything to use a tenant representative?

In most GTA industrial lease transactions the landlord pays the commission, so tenants typically do not pay their representative directly. Terms are confirmed in a written representation agreement before work begins.

Are subleases included in your search?

Yes. Lease searches on this site include sublease listings from the live MLS® feed. Subleases can offer shorter terms, existing improvements and lower rents, but they carry their own consent and term risks worth reviewing early.
