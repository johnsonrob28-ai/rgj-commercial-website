Before You Buy

What to check in a GTA industrial building.

Zoning & site

Permitted uses, outdoor storage, site coverage, expansion room, truck access and parking. A building that suits today but not your next five years is expensive to leave.

Condition & environmental

Roof, structure, mechanical systems and sprinklers, plus a Phase I environmental site assessment and a Phase II where the history calls for it.

Total cost of ownership

Purchase price, land transfer tax, financing, property taxes, insurance, capital repairs and fit-out, compared with leasing over the same period.

GTA industrial buying questions

How much do industrial buildings sell for in the GTA?

Colliers reported an average sale price of about $310 per square foot for freehold GTA industrial buildings of 15,000 SF and larger in Q3 2026, and about $477 per square foot for industrial condo units. Prices vary widely by city, building age, size, clear height, site coverage and condition, so compare specific buildings rather than relying on the average.

Should I buy or lease an industrial building?

Buying builds equity, fixes your occupancy cost and gives you control over the building, but ties up capital and leaves you responsible for capital repairs. Leasing preserves capital and flexibility. The right answer depends on how long you plan to stay, your financing and how specialized your space needs are; a side-by-side cost comparison over your holding period is the best place to start.

What is a sale-leaseback?

In a sale-leaseback, a business sells the building it occupies to an investor and signs a lease to stay. It frees up capital tied in real estate while the business keeps operating in place. Pricing depends on the lease term, rent and the tenant's covenant.

What should I check before buying an industrial building?

Confirm zoning and permitted uses, building condition and roof age, environmental reports (Phase I and, if needed, Phase II), power capacity, clear height, shipping, site coverage and outdoor storage, property taxes and any existing leases. Build these into your conditions period.

How long does it take to buy an industrial building?

Searching can take a few weeks to several months depending on supply for your size and area. Once an offer is accepted, conditional periods for financing, inspections and environmental review commonly run 30 to 90 days before closing.

Do you search off-market buildings?

Yes. Many owner-occupied and investment buildings trade without being widely advertised. Along with live MLS® listings, RGJ Commercial approaches owners directly when a requirement calls for it.
