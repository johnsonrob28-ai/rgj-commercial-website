How to read a cap rate

Cap rate = annual net operating income ÷ property value or purchase price. It measures property income before financing and income tax; it is not a mortgage rate or total investment return.

Lease term, tenant covenant, rent relative to market, building condition and location can affect pricing. These Toronto-market benchmarks are not separate rates for Mississauga, Brampton or other municipalities.
