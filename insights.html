Sources & methodology

Sale price, weighted average net asking rent, vacancy and availability: Colliers · Toronto Industrial Market Report Q3 2026, pages 1 and 3. Published October 5, 2026; verified November 14, 2026.

The sale figure is the published GTA freehold industrial average for buildings of 15,000 SF and larger; it is not an average total purchase price and excludes the separate condo/strata series. Rents are net asking rents per square foot per year. Vacancy measures unoccupied space; availability also includes space being marketed while occupied.

Cap rates: Colliers · Canada Cap Rate Report Q3 2026, Toronto table, page 5. Published October 22, 2026; verified November 14, 2026. These are published Class A and B ranges, not average cap rates from completed investment sales or separate municipal averages.

Cap rate = annual net operating income ÷ property value or purchase price. Building quality, location, tenant covenant and lease terms affect pricing. This snapshot is market context, not a property valuation.

Updated after each verified quarterly release. The last verified period stays visible until newer figures are confirmed. See the quarterly report for full definitions and coverage.

City views: for cities reported in several submarkets (for example Mississauga, Brampton, Etobicoke, Oakville and North York), each figure is a simple average of the published submarket figures, not a citywide figure published by the source. Submarkets without a published sale price are left out of the sale average. Cap rates are published for the Toronto market only and are the same for every city view.
