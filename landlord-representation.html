Common questions

How long does it take to lease industrial space?

It depends on size, location, building quality and pricing. Well-located, functional space priced to the market can lease within weeks; larger or specialized space can take longer. A clear strategy before launch shortens the timeline.

How is a landlord representative paid?

The landlord typically pays a leasing commission set out in the listing agreement, usually based on the lease value, and often shared with the tenant's brokerage. Terms are confirmed in writing before marketing begins.

What asking rent should I set?

Asking rent should reflect comparable available and recently leased space, the building's features and condition, and how quickly you want to lease. Use the Market Insight page for current GTA averages, and request a property-specific recommendation.

How are tenants qualified?

Prospective tenants are reviewed for fit with the space and permitted use, operating history and financial strength, with financial information requested where appropriate before a lease is finalized.

Can you help me keep my existing tenants?

Yes. Starting renewal discussions early, with a clear view of the market, often keeps good tenants in place and avoids downtime between leases.
