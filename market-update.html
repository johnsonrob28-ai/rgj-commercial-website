Sources, definitions & quarterly updates

How this dashboard is sourced

Primary series: Colliers · Q3 2026 · pages 1 & 3 ↗. Research publication: 2026-10-05. Selected published statistics are summarized here; the full report remains with its publisher. RGJ Commercial’s explanatory text is separate from the source data.

Cross-check: CBRE’s Q3 2026 report reports 5.0% availability. That is a separate series with different coverage and methodology—not a replacement value or a blended average.

Asking rent, availability and vacancy

Net asking rent is the advertised base rent before additional rent, taxes and operating costs; it is not an achieved lease rate. Availability includes marketed space that can still be occupied. Vacancy measures space recorded as vacant. Follow each publisher’s full definitions when comparing reports.

How to read sale prices by area

Sale prices are shown in CAD per building square foot using the publisher’s average-sale-price column. They are not average total purchase prices. Mississauga, Brampton and Etobicoke retain the publisher’s reporting subdivisions. Submarket averages are not combined into municipal averages without transaction weights.

A quarter with a different mix of condos, freehold buildings, investment properties or building sizes can produce a different average even when comparable property values have not moved. Compare the same geography and methodology over time, and use property-specific evidence for pricing decisions. A missing published value is unavailable, not zero.

Quarterly refresh policy

Check for new reports after each quarter closes. Keep the last verified quarter visible until a newer source is verified; never relabel old numbers. Archive each quarter’s data and record its source, publication and verification dates. No live MLS feed is required for these public-report summaries.
