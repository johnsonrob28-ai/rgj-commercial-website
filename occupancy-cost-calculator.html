How this is calculated
  • Totals cover the full lease term and are not discounted for the time value of money. HST is not included.
  • Free rent applies to net rent only; additional rent (TMI) is assumed to be payable throughout.
  • Rent increases apply at the start of each lease year, as a percentage or a fixed $/SF step. TMI increases by the percentage you enter each year.
  • The landlord allowance is deducted from your costs. Net effective rent is net rent less the allowance, spread over the term, per SF per year.
  • TMI, allowances and costs are estimates. This tool is for comparison only and is not financial, legal or tax advice.
