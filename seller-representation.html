Common questions

What is my industrial building worth?

Value depends on size, location, clear height, shipping, site coverage, condition, zoning and, for leased buildings, the income and lease terms. An opinion of value compares your building with recent comparable sales and current market conditions. Request one through the contact form.

How is a seller representative paid?

The seller typically pays a commission set out in the listing agreement, which often includes the share paid to a buyer's brokerage. Terms are confirmed in writing before marketing begins.

How long does it take to sell an industrial building?

Marketing commonly runs a few weeks to a few months depending on pricing, building type and demand, followed by a conditional period, often 30 to 90 days, before closing.

Should I sell publicly or off-market?

A public MLS® listing gives the widest exposure. A targeted off-market process can suit owners who value confidentiality, especially when the business or tenants are still operating. The right approach depends on your goals and the building.

Can I sell my building and stay in it?

Yes, through a sale-leaseback. You sell to an investor and sign a lease to remain, freeing up capital while operations continue. Pricing depends on the lease term, rent and your business's covenant.
