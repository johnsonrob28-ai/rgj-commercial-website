01

Sale-Leaseback Advisory

Explore releasing capital from your property while continuing to occupy it.

A sale-leaseback combines a property sale with a new lease for the operating business. We help owners assess whether the structure fits their plans and consider the sale price alongside the ongoing occupancy commitment.

How we can help

  • Clarify capital objectives and the business’s long-term space requirements.
  • Evaluate the relationship between sale proceeds, rent, lease term and future increases.
  • Position the opportunity for suitable investors and compare proposals.
  • Coordinate commercial negotiations with your legal, financing and tax advisers.

Industrial Investment Acquisitions & Dispositions

Evaluate income-producing assets and plan a purchase or disposition.

Investment decisions need a clear view of income, lease commitments and the property itself. We help buyers assess opportunities and owners prepare a disposition strategy that reflects the asset and their objectives.

How we can help

  • Review lease schedules, available operating information and comparable sales.
  • Compare pricing, income assumptions, vacancy exposure and capital requirements.
  • Develop acquisition criteria or a targeted disposition and buyer outreach plan.
  • Support offers, negotiations and due diligence coordination through closing.

Employment Land & Site Selection

Identify sites that suit your intended industrial use and growth plans.

Land suitability depends on more than acreage. We help narrow the search around location, intended use, access and servicing, then coordinate the questions that need to be answered before a commitment.
