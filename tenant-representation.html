Common questions

What does a tenant representative do?

A tenant representative works only for the tenant: defining the space requirement, searching the full market, touring and comparing options, negotiating the offer to lease and coordinating with your lawyer through to occupancy.

How much does tenant representation cost?

In most GTA industrial lease transactions the landlord pays the commission, so tenants typically do not pay their representative directly. Terms are confirmed in a written representation agreement before work begins.

When should I start planning a move or renewal?

For most industrial requirements, start 12 to 18 months before your lease expires or you need to occupy. Larger or specialized requirements should start earlier.

Can you help if I want to stay in my current building?

Yes. Testing the market before renewal talks often improves the renewal terms, and you keep the option to move if a better building is available.

Do you also represent landlords on the same deal?

RGJ Commercial will tell you up front if a property it represents for a landlord is a fit, and how that situation is handled, so you can decide how to proceed with full information.
